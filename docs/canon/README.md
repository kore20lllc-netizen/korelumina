# KoreLumina Canon

## Purpose

The Canon preserves the permanent identity of KoreLumina.

Unlike architecture, specifications, or implementation, the Canon defines the enduring truths upon which the entire engineering organization is built.

The Canon answers four questions:

- Who are we?
- Why do we exist?
- What principles govern us?
- What must never change?

Everything else in the repository derives its authority from these answers.

---

# Authority

The Canon is subordinate only to human leadership.

Within the repository, it represents the highest level of organizational knowledge.

Authority flows as follows.

```
Human Leadership
        ↓
Canon
        ↓
Constitution
        ↓
Blueprint
        ↓
Engineering Decision Records
        ↓
Architecture
        ↓
Specifications
        ↓
Implementation
```

No lower-level artifact may contradict the Canon.

---

# Purpose of the Canon

The Canon exists to preserve KoreLumina's identity across time.

Technology changes.

Programming languages evolve.

Artificial intelligence advances.

Products are replaced.

The Canon remains the enduring reference that ensures KoreLumina evolves without losing its identity.

---

# Canonical Documents

The Canon consists of a small collection of foundational documents.

## Founding Charter

Defines why KoreLumina exists.

---

## Manifesto

Defines the engineering philosophy that guides the organization.

---

## Vision 2050

Defines the long-term direction of the organization.

---

## Destiny

Defines the enduring purpose beyond products and technology.

---

## Constitution

Defines governance, authority, principles, and organizational structure.

---

# Characteristics of Canonical Documents

Canonical documents are:

- foundational
- long-lived
- organization-wide
- technology independent
- implementation independent
- strategically significant

Canonical documents are intentionally few.

---

# Governance

Canonical documents require constitutional governance.

Changes require:

- architectural review
- constitutional review
- approval by repository leadership
- documentation of rationale
- preservation of historical versions

Canonical documents are never casually modified.

---

# Relationship to the Constitution

The Canon defines identity.

The Constitution defines governance.

The Blueprint defines architecture.

Together they establish:

```
Identity

↓

Governance

↓

Architecture

↓

Execution
```

---

# Relationship to Engineering Decisions

Engineering Decision Records implement the Canon.

They do not redefine it.

EDRs explain architectural decisions.

The Canon explains organizational purpose.

---

# Relationship to the Knowledge Platform

The Knowledge Platform preserves operational knowledge.

The Canon preserves organizational identity.

Operational knowledge evolves continuously.

Canonical knowledge evolves deliberately.

---

# Relationship to the Genome

The KoreLumina Genome accumulates engineering capability over time.

The Canon provides the stable identity that guides that evolution.

The Genome may grow indefinitely.

The Canon should change only when the organization itself fundamentally evolves.

---

# Stewardship

The Canon is maintained on behalf of future contributors.

Every engineer and every AI operating within KoreLumina inherits the Canon before contributing to the platform.

The Canon exists to ensure that future innovation strengthens KoreLumina rather than redefining it unintentionally.

---

# Canon Principle

The Canon is the permanent identity of KoreLumina.

Everything else may evolve.

The identity of the organization must remain coherent across generations of engineers, technologies, and implementations.
