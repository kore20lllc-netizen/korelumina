# Purpose

The Validation Workbench is the operational heart of Knowledge Operations.

Every piece of organizational knowledge must pass through this workbench
before becoming trusted organizational memory.

The workbench is not an approval screen.

It is an investigative environment.

Its objective is not speed.

Its objective is organizational truth.

---

# 1. Mission

The Validation Workbench exists to answer one question.

"Should this knowledge become part of the organization's permanent memory?"

Everything in the workbench supports answering that question.

---

# 2. Core Principles

Validation is evidence driven.

Validation is explainable.

Validation is auditable.

Validation is collaborative.

Validation is repeatable.

Validation never destroys history.

Validation produces organizational trust.

---

# 3. Operational Goals

The workbench enables operators to:

inspect candidate knowledge

evaluate evidence

review provenance

detect conflicts

compare competing claims

understand impact

record reasoning

approve trusted knowledge

reject invalid knowledge

request additional evidence

merge related knowledge

split ambiguous knowledge

promote canonical knowledge

The Validation Workbench is certified only when:

• Every decision is evidence-based.

• Every decision is explainable.

• Every decision is auditable.

• Every candidate exposes complete provenance.

• Relationships remain visible.

• Confidence is transparent.

• Human reasoning becomes organizational knowledge.

• Canonical promotion is deliberate.

• Rejection preserves history.

• Operators can confidently certify organizational truth.
