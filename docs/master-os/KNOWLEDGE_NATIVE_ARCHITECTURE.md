# Knowledge-Native Architecture

## Purpose

This document establishes the mandatory architectural principles for every subsystem developed after Architecture Baseline 1.0.

Beginning with KEP-002, KoreLumina becomes a Knowledge-Native Engineering Platform.

Knowledge is no longer a feature.

Knowledge becomes infrastructure.

---

# Principle

Every subsystem shall participate in the organizational knowledge ecosystem.

Every subsystem is both:

- a producer of knowledge
- a consumer of knowledge

No production subsystem shall exist in isolation.

---

# Mandatory Capabilities

Every new subsystem shall define:

- Capability ID
- Capability Owner
- Capability Maturity
- Dependencies
- Produced Knowledge
- Consumed Knowledge
- Events Published
- Events Consumed

These become part of the Capability Registry.

---

# Repository Intelligence

Every subsystem shall expose:

- repository artifacts
- architectural metadata
- implementation metadata
- operational metadata

Repository Intelligence becomes the discovery layer of KoreLumina.

---

# Knowledge Operations

Every subsystem shall support:

- knowledge capture
- knowledge indexing
- knowledge retrieval
- knowledge evolution

Knowledge Operations becomes the processing layer.

---

# Knowledge Graph

Every subsystem shall establish relationships between:

- projects
- repositories
- capabilities
- missions
- Engineering Decision Records
- architectural components
- runtime components
- contributors

The Knowledge Graph becomes the relationship layer.

---

# Organizational Memory

Every subsystem shall preserve:

- important events
- engineering decisions
- architectural evolution
- operational history
- recovery history
- mission outcomes

Organizational Memory becomes the historical layer.

---

# Genome Contribution

Every subsystem shall contribute to the KoreLumina Genome.

Typical contributions include:

- engineering patterns
- operational knowledge
- architectural improvements
- capability maturity
- organizational learning

No subsystem is exempt.

---

# Chief Agent Visibility

Every subsystem shall expose information consumable by the Chief Agent.

The Chief Agent should always be capable of answering:

- What exists?
- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- What knowledge was created?
- What capability improved?
- What risks exist?
- What should happen next?

---

# Observability

Every subsystem shall expose:

- health
- metrics
- diagnostics
- lifecycle
- events

Knowledge without observability is incomplete.

---

# Traceability

Every implementation shall remain traceable to:

Constitution

↓

Blueprint

↓

Capability

↓

Engineering Decision

↓

Implementation

↓

Knowledge Produced

↓

Genome Update

Traceability is mandatory.

---

# Knowledge-Native Rule

Beginning with KEP-002, every capability developed within KoreLumina shall participate in the Knowledge Platform by producing, consuming, preserving, and evolving organizational knowledge.

Knowledge is infrastructure.

The Knowledge Platform is the nervous system of KoreLumina.
