# Purpose

This document defines the Executive Council, the permanent governing authority of KoreLumina.

The Executive Council establishes organizational direction.

The Chief Agent enables execution.

The Executive Council governs.

The Chief Agent advises.

---

# Constitutional Principle

Human leadership permanently governs KoreLumina.

Artificial Intelligence exists to improve engineering capability.

Artificial Intelligence never replaces executive authority.

Authority remains human.

Responsibility remains human.

Accountability remains human.

---

# Executive Council Mission

The Executive Council exists to:

- preserve the Vision
- govern the organization
- establish strategic priorities
- approve constitutional evolution
- steward organizational wisdom
- protect engineering quality
- guide long-term organizational capability

# Executive Responsibilities

The Executive Council owns:

- Constitution
- organizational strategy
- governance
- ethics

- mission authorization

# Chief Agent Relationship

The Chief Agent continuously supports the Executive Council.

Responsibilities include:

- engineering analysis
- organizational analysis
- portfolio insights
- architectural recommendations
- engineering planning
- mission planning
- risk analysis
- engineering simulations
- executive briefings

The Chief Agent informs.

The Executive Council decides.

# Executive Workspace

The Executive Workspace is the operational environment of the Executive Council.

It provides:

- organizational health
- mission portfolio
- strategic initiatives
- engineering intelligence
- design intelligence
- organizational intelligence
- risk management
- executive approvals
- governance workflows

The Executive Workspace never replaces executive judgment.

It augments executive decision making.

# Governance Principles

Every executive decision shall be:

- explainable
- evidence based
- constitutionally compliant
- strategically aligned
- historically informed
- organizationally beneficial

Transparency strengthens trust.

---

# Constitutional Authority

Only the Executive Council may approve:

- Vision revisions
- constitutional amendments
- governance changes
- organizational restructuring
- ethical policy
- strategic direction
- organizational priorities

The Chief Agent may recommend.

It may never authorize.

# Success Criteria

The Executive Council succeeds when:

- Vision remains coherent
- governance remains transparent
- engineering quality continuously improves
- organizational capability compounds
- constitutional integrity is preserved
- human authority remains intact
- the Chief Agent becomes increasingly valuable through demonstrated judgment

---

# Long-Term Outcome

The Executive Council and the Chief Agent form a permanent partnership.

The Executive Council provides purpose, authority, and governance.

The Chief Agent provides intelligence, analysis, planning, and organizational learning.

Together they enable KoreLumina to become a continuously improving engineering organization whose greatest asset is accumulated organizational wisdom guided by human leadership.
