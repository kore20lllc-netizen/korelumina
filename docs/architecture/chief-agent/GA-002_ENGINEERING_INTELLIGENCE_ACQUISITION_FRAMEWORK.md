Status: Draft

Authority: Vision 2050 Alignment

Depends On:

---

# Purpose

This document defines the canonical framework for acquiring Engineering Intelligence throughout the lifetime of KoreLumina.

Knowledge is not manually entered.

Knowledge is continuously acquired from engineering activity.

The objective is to ensure that every engineering action has the opportunity to improve the organization and the Chief Agent.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering activity is the raw material.

Engineering Intelligence is the finished product.

Knowledge acquisition is therefore a manufacturing process rather than a documentation process.

---

# Objectives

The framework shall:

- continuously acquire engineering evidence
- preserve provenance
- reconstruct engineering context
- validate candidate knowledge
- prevent architectural drift
- produce reusable Engineering Intelligence
- continuously educate the Chief Agent

# Provenance

Every knowledge item shall permanently preserve:

- origin
- timestamp
- author
- source artifact
- supporting evidence
- confidence
- validation history
- supersession history

Knowledge without provenance shall never become canonical.

---

# Confidence

Every candidate receives a confidence assessment.

Typical contributors include:

- evidence quality
- corroboration
- engineering validation
- architectural consistency
- runtime verification
- historical agreement

Confidence may increase or decrease over time as additional evidence becomes available.

---

# Validation

Knowledge progresses through defined stages.

Observed

↓

Candidate

↓

Reviewed

↓

Validated

↓

Canonical

↓

Historical

Only Canonical knowledge may directly influence organizational guidance and Chief Agent reasoning.

---

# Conflict Resolution

Conflicting knowledge shall never overwrite existing knowledge automatically.

Conflicts create review workflows.

Possible outcomes include:

- merge
- supersede
- reject
- preserve as historical variant

Engineering history must remain explainable.

---

# Versioning

Knowledge evolves.

Every canonical artifact shall preserve:

- version
- effective date
- superseded version
- rationale
- migration history

Nothing is permanently deleted.

---

# Assimilation

Canonical knowledge becomes Engineering Intelligence through assimilation.

Assimilation includes:

- pattern extraction
- architectural understanding
- design understanding
- engineering heuristics
- recovery intelligence
- governance reasoning

The objective is judgment rather than memorization.

# Design Intelligence

Design knowledge follows the same lifecycle.

Evidence includes:

- component evolution
- interaction patterns
- accessibility
- typography
- spacing
- visual hierarchy
- motion
- usability findings

Validated design knowledge becomes reusable organizational guidance.

---

# Success Criteria

The framework succeeds when:

- engineering knowledge is continuously captured
- provenance is never lost
- knowledge remains explainable
- conflicts are governed
- engineering capability compounds over time
- design intelligence continuously improves
- the Chief Agent demonstrates increasingly mature engineering judgment

---

# Long-Term Outcome

Engineering Intelligence becomes a continuously growing organizational asset.

Rather than relying on individual memory, KoreLumina develops a durable institutional understanding that spans projects, teams, and generations.

The Chief Agent becomes more capable because the organization continuously learns from itself under human governance.
