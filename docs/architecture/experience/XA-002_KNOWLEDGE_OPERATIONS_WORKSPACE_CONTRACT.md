# XA-002 — Knowledge Operations Workspace Contract

Status: Draft

Authority:

- XA-001 Knowledge Operations Experience Architecture

---

# Principle

The workspace is the visible representation of the Engineering Intelligence Platform.

Every visible surface corresponds to a real platform capability.

The UI is the contract.

---

# First Impression

Knowledge Operations should immediately communicate:

This organization is learning.

The user should never feel they are looking at a database.

They should feel they are observing a living engineering organization.

---

# Layout

The workspace consists of five persistent regions.

──────────────────────────────────────────────

Executive Intelligence Header

──────────────────────────────────────────────

Strategic Intelligence Bar

──────────────────────────────────────────────

Operational Navigation

──────────────────────────────────────────────

Primary Intelligence Canvas

──────────────────────────────────────────────

Context & Intelligence Inspector

──────────────────────────────────────────────

No floating dashboards.

No disconnected widgets.

Everything belongs to a coherent intelligence workflow.

---

# Executive Intelligence Header

Always visible.

Displays:

• Organizational Health

• Knowledge Growth

• Engineering Intelligence Growth

• Chief Agent Learning Status

• Active Reviews

• Organizational Confidence

• Mission Intelligence

This answers:

"How healthy is KoreLumina?"

# Success Criteria

A new engineer should understand:

what the organization knows

what changed

why it changed

who validated it

how it affects engineering

what the Chief Agent learned

within minutes of opening the workspace.

The interface should feel less like software and more like observing the collective engineering mind of KoreLumina.
