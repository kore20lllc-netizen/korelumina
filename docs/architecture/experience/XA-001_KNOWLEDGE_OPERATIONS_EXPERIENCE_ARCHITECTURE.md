Status: Draft

Authority:

---

# Principle

The UI is the contract.

Knowledge Operations is not designed from components.

It is designed from organizational capabilities.

Every visible surface represents a real capability.

No decorative views.

No placeholder dashboards.

No fake metrics.

No isolated widgets.

Every interaction must correspond to actual organizational intelligence.

---

# Mission

Provide the primary operating environment for Engineering Intelligence.

Users should feel they are operating the collective engineering intelligence of KoreLumina.

---

# Experience Goals

Knowledge Operations shall provide:

• complete engineering visibility

• complete explainability

• minimal cognitive load

• premium aesthetics

• executive clarity

• production reliability

• engineering confidence

• organizational awareness

---

# Workspace Philosophy

The workspace is organized by intelligence flow rather than navigation hierarchy.

Engineering Activity

↓

Evidence

↓

Knowledge

↓

Engineering Intelligence

↓

Chief Agent Learning

↓

Organizational Wisdom

Navigation follows organizational cognition.

---

# Primary Workspace

Knowledge Operations opens into an Executive Intelligence Workspace.

This is not a dashboard.

It is the operational center of organizational learning.

The opening experience immediately answers:

What changed?

What was learned?

What requires review?

What is waiting?

What intelligence was created today?

What became canonical?

What is the Chief Agent currently learning?

---

---

# Design Principles

Every screen shall be:

Explainable.

Traceable.

Actionable.

Consistent.

Evidence-based.

Fast.

Elegant.

Readable.

Premium.

---

# Information Hierarchy

Critical intelligence first.

Operational context second.

Supporting evidence third.

Historical detail on demand.

The user should never need to search for the next action.

---

# Explainability

Every intelligence object exposes:

Origin.

Evidence.

Confidence.

Relationships.

Validation.

History.

Consumers.

Nothing appears without provenance.

---

# Visual Language

The interface communicates:

confidence

maturity

relationships

priority

health

learning

governance

status

without overwhelming the user.

---

# Success Criteria

The workspace succeeds when a user can understand the engineering state of KoreLumina within minutes, trace every conclusion back to its evidence, and confidently act on validated organizational intelligence.
