# Purpose

This document defines the Intelligence Framework of KoreLumina.

Knowledge alone does not create capability.

Capability emerges when validated knowledge is transformed into reusable organizational intelligence.

The Intelligence Framework defines every intelligence domain within KoreLumina and how those domains interact.

---

# Foundational Principle

Intelligence is not data.

Intelligence is validated understanding that continuously improves organizational capability.

Every intelligence domain exists to strengthen human decision making.

Knowledge Operations is responsible for:

• acquiring intelligence

• validating intelligence

• relating intelligence

# Chief Agent

# Success Criteria

The Intelligence Framework succeeds when:

• every intelligence domain continuously improves

• organizational capability compounds

• executive decisions become increasingly informed

• engineering quality increases

• design quality increases

• organizational learning accelerates

---

# Long-Term Outcome

KoreLumina develops a continuously expanding intelligence ecosystem where every engineering activity strengthens one or more intelligence domains, every intelligence domain strengthens organizational capability, and every organizational improvement advances the Vision under human governance.
