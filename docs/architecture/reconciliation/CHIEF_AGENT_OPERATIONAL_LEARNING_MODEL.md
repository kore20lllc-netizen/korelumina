## 2. Integrated System Purpose

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The Knowledge Constitution defines how engineering evidence becomes governed organizational knowledge: Evidence → Knowledge IR → Knowledge Package → Canonical Knowledge → Organizational Memory. Authority: CA-001 through CA-004.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The Learning Constitution defines how the Chief Agent is educated before activation and how learning continues afterward: Historical Replay → Genesis Corpus → Repository Knowledge Seeding → Educational Corpus → Initial Competency → Human Activation → Continuous Learning. Authority: CA-005.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The Chief Agent architecture defines the executive operating loop `Observe → Understand → Plan → Delegate → Monitor → Recover → Learn → Improve`, grounded in Runtime truth and subject to human approval. Authority: `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, sections **Primary Responsibilities**, **Relationship to Runtime**, **Decision Boundaries**, and **Human Override**.

**INFERENCE** — The integrated operational model has five distinct constitutional functions:

- **Education** establishes pre-activation competency from approved curriculum and governed historical knowledge.
- **Learning** converts post-activation validated experience into patterns, insights, memory, and improved future capability.
- **Reasoning** interprets governed context and produces explainable findings or recommendations.
- **Planning** converts approved objectives and reasoning into executable mission structures.
- **Execution** belongs to delegated agents and Runtime-governed systems; it does not belong to knowledge or memory subsystems.

## 4. Education Versus Continuous Learning

### Pre-activation education

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Education precedes activation. Authority: CA-005, **New wording** and Articles II, VIII, and IX.

Pre-activation education includes:

- governed historical reconstruction;
- Genesis corpus assembly;
- repository knowledge seeding;
- constitutional and architectural curriculum;
- canonical knowledge and Organizational Memory projections;
- initial competency assessment;
- human acceptance of certification.

### Post-activation learning

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Continuous Learning begins after activation and may consume validated mission, engineering, Runtime, deployment, incident, conversation, recovery, and operational evidence. Authority: CA-005, Articles II and X.

**INFERENCE** — The constitutional boundary is the activation decision. Before activation, material is curriculum or governed educational knowledge. After activation, new experience is evidence that must pass through the Knowledge Constitution before it may influence future reasoning or planning as trusted learning.

## 6. First Production Learning Cycle

The smallest constitutionally valid production cycle is:

1. **Approved mission or learning event.** The mission is identified, owned, scoped, and approved under the Mission System.
2. **Governed context.** Context is assembled from authorized Canonical Knowledge, Organizational Memory, current mission state, and required Runtime facts.
3. **Execution or observed engineering activity.** A bounded task is executed by an authorized agent or human, or an engineering event is observed.
4. **Runtime truth where applicable.** Operational claims are verified against Runtime state, telemetry, lifecycle events, logs, validation, or recovery outcomes.
5. **Evidence capture.** Source identity, time, content reference, checksum, metadata, and relationships are preserved.
6. **Compilation into Knowledge IR.** An evidence-specific compiler emits provisional candidate knowledge.
7. **Validation.** Required fields, evidence references, confidence basis, conflicts, and human-review requirements are evaluated.
8. **Knowledge Package creation.** Validated IR, evidence, provenance, scope, confidence, relationships, lifecycle state, and intended projections are assembled into the governed package.
9. **Canonical review and approval.** Human review and approval occur where required; validation alone is insufficient for canonical authority.
10. **Organizational Memory adaptation.** Approved knowledge is generalized, privacy-filtered, validated, and projected while preserving lineage and authority.
11. **Chief Agent consumption.** The Chief Agent receives scoped, provenance-backed knowledge or memory as decision input, not Runtime truth or canonical-promotion authority.
12. **Learning-effect validation.** A later reasoning or planning outcome must visibly cite, apply, or change because of the newly governed knowledge.

Required human checkpoints:

- mission approval where required;
- high-impact execution approval;
- canonical promotion approval;
- activation and educational changes;
- acceptance of learning-effect evidence where it changes standards, architecture, authority, or autonomous scope.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Human approval is required for architecture changes, destructive actions, production deployment, repository rewrites, autonomous execution expansion, recovery rollback, and canonical promotion. Authority: `docs/chief-agent/CHIEF_AGENT_OPERATING_MODEL.md`, section **Human Approval Gates**.

## 8. Bounded-Context Ownership

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Knowledge Operations and the Chief Agent workspace are separate bounded contexts. The Chief Agent coordinates Knowledge Operations as a managed system; it does not absorb it. Authority: `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, sections **Managed Systems** and **Relationship to the Knowledge Platform**; `CHIEF_AGENT_INTERFACE.md`, sections **Knowledge Center**, **Memory Center**, and **Execution Center**.

- **Knowledge Operations** owns observability and governed operation of evidence, IR, packages, validation, canonical review, adaptation, provenance, and lineage.
- **Chief Agent Workspace / Executive Office** owns executive briefing, mission intake, priorities, reasoning, planning, approvals, orchestration, and operational visibility.
- **KPP** owns evidence-to-knowledge processing and preservation; it does not own execution.
- **Canonical Knowledge** owns authoritative knowledge state within scope; human governance owns promotion authority.
- **Organizational Memory** owns governed stewardship and retrieval of adapted canonical projections; it does not grant canonical status.
- **Educational Corpus** is governed curriculum custody; it does not own Runtime state or post-activation learning evidence.
- **Historical Replay** is a Knowledge Platform recovery and education process; it does not execute missions.
- **Runtime Operations** owns operational truth, process lifecycle, telemetry, logs, incidents, and recovery state.
- **Mission System** owns mission lifecycle and traceability from objective through knowledge extraction.
- **Engineer Agent** owns bounded engineering task competency under delegated authority.
- **Human governance** owns constitutional direction, curriculum approval, activation, high-impact approvals, canonical promotion, and accountability.

## 9. Chief Agent and Engineer Agent Relationship

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The Platform Constitution defines an Engineer Agent growth model. Approved later architecture defines the Chief Agent as executive governor and mission owner. No governing document explicitly states that one supersedes the other. Authority: Platform Constitution Law 8; `CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`; `CHIEF_AGENT_LEARNING_RECONCILIATION.md`, recorded conflict.

**INFERENCE** — The reconciled relationship is:

- both consume a shared governed educational substrate;
- the Engineer Agent owns bounded engineering competency and task execution capability;
- the Chief Agent owns executive understanding, mission planning, orchestration, delegation, monitoring, recovery, learning, and improvement;
- specialist and Engineer Agents receive delegated tasks, not mission ownership;
- human approval remains authoritative;
- Runtime remains execution truth;
- both return evidence and learning outputs to the Knowledge Platform.

**UNRESOLVED** — Runtime delegation contracts, competency inheritance, authority escalation, specialist-agent creation thresholds, and whether the Chief Agent directly contains or merely coordinates the Engineer Agent remain undefined.

## 10. Runtime Operations Role

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Runtime answers “What is executing?” and is authoritative for running state. Authority: `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, section **Relationship to Runtime**.

Runtime Operations contributes:

- Runtime truth for operational claims;
- telemetry and lifecycle events;
- logs and diagnostic evidence;
- incidents and recovery evidence;
- operator actions and approval records;
- execution outcomes and validation results;
- compiler inputs for incident, recovery, operational, and execution knowledge.

**INFERENCE** — Runtime outputs enter learning only as evidence. They must be captured, compiled, validated, packaged, reviewed where required, and adapted through the Knowledge Constitution before becoming trusted organizational learning.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Runtime Operations is not Organizational Memory, Canonical Knowledge, Knowledge Operations, or the Chief Agent. Platform Constitution Law 7 states KP does not own execution; Chief Agent architecture preserves Runtime truth and distinct managed systems.

## 11. Knowledge Operations Role

Knowledge Operations is the observable operating center for:

- evidence intake and source status;
- compiler registration and execution state;
- Knowledge IR lifecycle;
- Knowledge Package assembly and lifecycle;
- validation results and review requirements;
- canonical review, approval, rejection, supersession, and publication state;
- Organizational Memory adaptation and validation;
- provenance and evidence references;
- lineage across Evidence → IR → Package → Canonical → Memory;
- knowledge-flow health and blocked transitions.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Knowledge Operations workspace specifications require promotion governance, evidence review, canonical exploration, and immutable evidence visibility. CA-001 through CA-004 require complete traceability and visible lifecycle state.

**INFERENCE** — Every Knowledge Package must be visibly traceable from source evidence through validation, review, canonical decision, adaptation, consumer projection, and lifecycle outcome. This is an operational requirement, not a UI design decision.

## 14. Implementation Stages

### Stage 1 — Authority and Registry Foundations

Purpose: make authority, curriculum, activation, canonical review, and lifecycle identities explicit.

Dependencies: completed constitutional reconciliation.

Components: Curriculum Registry, Educational Corpus Registry, Activation Authority Registry, package and canonical authority contracts.

Acceptance: every governed artifact has identity, authority, status, owner, version, scope, and lifecycle.

Completion boundary: no educational or canonical transition depends on implicit authority.

### Stage 2 — Historical Replay and Genesis

Purpose: reconstruct and certify the historical source corpus.

Dependencies: Stage 1.

Components: Historical Replay Engine, replay identity/checkpoints/deduplication, Genesis Corpus Registry, Repository Knowledge Seeder.

Acceptance: replay is repeatable, provenance-preserving, conflict-aware, and produces a versioned Genesis corpus.

Completion boundary: Genesis corpus certification evidence exists.

### Stage 3 — Compiler and Knowledge Package Completion

Purpose: complete evidence-class coverage and governed package assembly.

Dependencies: Stages 1–2.

Components: Documentation, Conversation, Runtime, Mission, and Execution compilers; IR validation; Knowledge Package Registry.

Acceptance: each required evidence class produces traceable IR and packages with review requirements.

Completion boundary: no required learning source bypasses Evidence → IR → Package.

### Stage 4 — Governed Canonical and Memory Flow

Purpose: align implementation with the Knowledge Constitution.

Dependencies: Stage 3.

Components: human canonical review workflow, approval recording, canonical lifecycle, Organizational Memory adaptation metadata and validation.

Acceptance: automatic promotion cannot bypass required approval; lineage and privacy survive adaptation.

Completion boundary: end-to-end package-to-memory trace is certified.

### Stage 5 — Educational Corpus and Competency

Purpose: assemble curriculum and prove pre-activation competency.

Dependencies: Stages 1–4; Graph, Search, and Context foundations.

Components: Educational Corpus Registry, curriculum admission, Competency Evaluator, certification evidence.

Acceptance: competency can be reproduced, reviewed, accepted, and versioned.

Completion boundary: human-approved competency certificate exists.

### Stage 6 — Chief Agent Activation and Mission Operation

Purpose: activate the executive loop under human and Runtime authority.

Dependencies: Stage 5; Reasoning and Planning foundations; delegation and Runtime observation.

Components: Chief Agent Runtime, mission intake, governed context, reasoning, planning, approvals, delegation, observation.

Acceptance: one approved mission executes with visible ownership, Runtime grounding, validation, and rollback.

Completion boundary: activated Chief Agent completes one non-learning mission safely.

### Stage 7 — First Production Learning Cycle

Purpose: prove post-activation learning changes future capability.

Dependencies: Stage 6 and full Knowledge Constitution flow.

Components: evidence capture, compilers, package lifecycle, canonical review, memory adaptation, learning pipeline, learning-effect validation.

Acceptance: a later reasoning or planning decision measurably changes because of newly governed mission knowledge.

Completion boundary: first production learning cycle is certified and reproducible.

### Stage 8 — Continuous Learning Operations

Purpose: operate learning continuously without knowledge drift or authority erosion.

Dependencies: Stage 7.

Components: feedback loop, recertification, replay, monitoring, Knowledge Operations observability, supersession and retirement propagation.

Acceptance: repeated cycles preserve trust, privacy, provenance, Runtime truth, and human governance.

Completion boundary: continuous-learning health and governance are operationally observable.

**CONFLICT** — Repository Knowledge Seeding places implementation above Constitution in one precedence list, contrary to Constitutional Documentation Governance and CA-002.

**CONFLICT** — Some historical documents call the Knowledge Graph authoritative memory; the Constitution assigns permanent memory to KP and CA-002 assigns canonical authority to Canonical Knowledge.

## 17. Amendment Candidates

**INFERENCE** — No new constitutional amendment is presently required for the integrated operational model. CA-001 through CA-005 establish the governing principles for knowledge, memory, education, activation, and learning.

Potential future constitutional work exists only if implementation planning proves that one of these remains a genuine missing principle rather than a mechanism:

- constitutional ownership of canonical revocation, supersession, retirement, and propagation;
- constitutional resolution of Engineer Agent ↔ Chief Agent identity and delegated authority, if approved architecture cannot resolve it;
- constitutional recertification or deactivation authority, if existing human-governance principles prove insufficient.

Replay engines, registries, compiler implementations, certification schemas, and UI observability are implementation mechanisms, not amendment candidates.
