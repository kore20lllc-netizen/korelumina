# CA-005 — Learning Constitution

## Amendment identifier

`CA-005`

## Summary

This amendment establishes the Learning Constitution of KoreLumina.

It defines how the Chief Agent is educated before activation and how governed learning continues after activation. It consolidates Genesis, Historical Replay, Repository Knowledge Seeding, Documentation Education, Initial Competency, Continuous Learning, and the Engineer Agent–Chief Agent educational relationship into one constitutional model.

It does not redesign the Knowledge Platform, Organizational Memory, Runtime, the Chief Agent, the Engineer Agent, or the Knowledge Constitution.

## Previous wording

The repository defined historical recovery, repository knowledge seeding, initial engineering competency, continuous learning, mission learning, and agent improvement across multiple constitutional, architectural, reconciliation, and implementation documents. It did not contain a single constitutional lifecycle separating pre-activation education from post-activation learning.

## New wording

The Chief Agent shall be educated before activation.

Education is the governed pre-activation lifecycle through which historical evidence, constitutional authority, approved architecture, validated organizational knowledge, and institutional memory are assembled into an Educational Corpus and validated into Initial Competency.

Learning begins after activation. Continuous Learning is the governed post-activation lifecycle through which validated mission, engineering, runtime, deployment, incident, conversation, and operational evidence improves future capability without bypassing canonical governance, human authority, or Runtime truth.

# Article I — Purpose

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/canon/VISION_2050.md`, sections **Our Long-Term Vision**, **Organizational Evolution**, **Engineering Intelligence**, **Human Leadership**, and **The KoreLumina Promise**, requires permanent institutional memory, compounding engineering capability, responsible artificial intelligence under permanent human governance, and cross-generation organizational improvement.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`, Laws 6–12, requires engineering evidence to become reusable knowledge, historical work to be recovered into KP, agent growth through demonstrated evidence, complete traceability through learning, and every task to produce both platform capability and learning.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/reconciliation/LEARNING_CONSTITUTION_RECONCILIATION.md`, sections **Genesis**, **Day-0 Learning**, **Complete Educational Lifecycle**, **One Learning Constitution or Separate Amendments?**, and **Dependencies**, reconciles historical recovery, seeding, initial competency, continuous learning, and agent education into one constitutional model with distinct phases.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Learning Constitution exists to ensure that the Chief Agent never begins from empty memory, never enters operation without governed education, and never treats post-activation experience as authoritative knowledge without the evidence, validation, review, canonical, memory, and human-governance controls established by the Knowledge Constitution.

# Article II — Educational Lifecycle

```text
Historical Replay
↓
Genesis Corpus
↓
Repository Knowledge Seeding
↓
Educational Corpus
↓
Initial Competency
↓
Activation
↓
Continuous Learning
```

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_REPOSITORY_KNOWLEDGE_SEEDING_V1.md`, sections **Purpose**, **Git History Recovery**, **Engineer Learning Lifecycle**, **Continuous Knowledge Synchronization**, and **Long-Term Evolution**, defines historical repository recovery as the means of establishing initial agent competency before autonomous learning and then continuing cumulative learning throughout the lifetime of KoreLumina.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/reconciliation/LEARNING_CONSTITUTION_RECONCILIATION.md`, section **Complete Educational Lifecycle of the Chief Agent**, reconciles historical evidence, recovery, Genesis corpus, Knowledge IR, Knowledge Package, Canonical Knowledge, Organizational Memory, graph, retrieval, context, Day-0 Learning, initial competency, activation, mission operation, and continuous learning into one lifecycle.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Historical Replay reconstructs prior activity. The Genesis Corpus is the governed historical source set produced from that reconstruction. Repository Knowledge Seeding converts the recoverable historical source set into structured, validated organizational knowledge. The Educational Corpus combines governed institutional curriculum with validated historical knowledge. Initial Competency is the validated capability state derived from that corpus. Activation is the human-authorized transition into operational responsibility. Continuous Learning begins only after activation.

# Article III — Historical Replay

## Purpose

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Historical Replay is the governed chronological reconstruction and reprocessing of KoreLumina’s prior engineering activity for educational use.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/canon/VISION_2050.md`, section **Age V — Legacy**, identifies Mission Replay, Historical Archive, and cross-generation knowledge transfer as enduring capabilities.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_REPOSITORY_KNOWLEDGE_SEEDING_V1.md`, sections **Git History Recovery**, **Historical Capability Recovery**, **Architecture Recovery**, **Regression Knowledge Recovery**, and **Production Incident Recovery**, requires recovery of capabilities, decisions, rationale, failures, incidents, lessons, and engineering evolution rather than only the latest implementation.

## Ownership

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Historical Replay belongs to the Knowledge Preservation and Knowledge Platform boundary as a recovery and education process. It does not belong to Runtime execution, Organizational Memory canonical authority, or Chief Agent operational control.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`, Law 7, assigns permanent engineering memory to KP and states that KP does not own execution.

## Governance

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Replay must preserve source identity, chronology, provenance, conflicts, superseded approaches, failed approaches, and governing authority. Human governance approves the replay scope used for education and any exclusion of material constitutional to competency.

## Boundaries

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Historical Replay is a process, not Canonical Knowledge, Organizational Memory, Runtime state, or the learning result. Replay may reconstruct historical evidence and context but may not grant canonical status or fabricate unavailable historical state.

**UNRESOLVED** — Replay identity, checkpoint semantics, deduplication rules, and exact-order guarantees remain constitutionally undefined beyond the principles established here.

# Article IV — Genesis Corpus

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Genesis Corpus is the governed, provenance-preserving source set assembled through Historical Replay for pre-activation education.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`, Law 9, requires git history, recovered branches, previous fixes, failed approaches, architecture evolution, runtime recovery, deployment evolution, AI evolution, prompt evolution, and engineering discussions to be recovered and fed into KP.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_REPOSITORY_KNOWLEDGE_SEEDING_V1.md`, section **Repository Knowledge Sources**, includes git history, architecture documentation, ADRs, RFCs, specifications, engineering standards, governance, roadmaps, reconciliations, incidents, runtime telemetry, deployment history, regressions, postmortems, source code, production commits, issue resolutions, and capability implementations.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, section **Relationship to the Knowledge Platform**, identifies architecture documents, specifications, engineering decisions, playbooks, runtime events, git history, conversations, and Organizational Memory as Chief Agent knowledge inputs.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Genesis Corpus may include repository history, historical decisions, architectural evolution, specifications, validated conversations, ADRs, EDRs, mission evolution, runtime and deployment evidence, incidents, recoveries, audits, reconciliations, standards, governance, and other constitutionally permitted historical sources. Inclusion does not itself grant canonical authority; each source remains subject to the Evidence, Knowledge IR, Knowledge Package, Canonical Knowledge, and Organizational Memory contracts.

# Article V — Repository Knowledge Seeding

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_REPOSITORY_KNOWLEDGE_SEEDING_V1.md`, section **Purpose**, defines Repository Knowledge Seeding as the authoritative process that converts repository history into permanent engineering knowledge and establishes initial agent competency before autonomous learning begins.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The same document, sections **Knowledge Extraction Standards**, **Knowledge Validation**, **Knowledge Graph Integration**, and **Engineer Learning Lifecycle**, requires structured, machine-readable, traceable, validated records before consumption by the agent.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Repository Knowledge Seeding is the governed initialization process that transforms the Genesis Corpus into validated educational knowledge through evidence capture, extraction, normalization, validation, canonical governance, Organizational Memory stewardship, graph publication, retrieval, and context construction.

**CONFLICT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_REPOSITORY_KNOWLEDGE_SEEDING_V1.md`, section **Repository as the Authoritative Knowledge Source**, places implementation above Constitution and approved architecture. Constitutional Documentation Governance and CA-002 require Constitution and governing architecture to remain higher authority. This amendment does not resolve that document conflict; it preserves the higher authority order.

# Article VI — Educational Corpus

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Educational Corpus is the approved pre-activation curriculum used to establish Initial Competency. It is not a dump of all repository artifacts and is not operational evidence by itself.

## Required constitutional and organizational curriculum

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/canon/VISION_2050.md` defines enduring organizational purpose, human leadership, institutional memory, and organizational evolution. It belongs in the Educational Corpus as supreme curriculum.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md` defines mandatory platform laws, learning obligations, traceability, production standards, and agent growth. It belongs in the Educational Corpus as constitutional curriculum.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/governance/CONSTITUTIONAL_DOCUMENT_GOVERNANCE.md`, sections **Authority Classes**, **Approval States**, **Ownership**, **Supersession Rules**, and **Conflict Resolution Procedure**, defines how documents govern and how conflicts are resolved. Governance belongs in the Educational Corpus.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/amendments/CA-001_KNOWLEDGE_PACKAGE.md`, `CA-002_CANONICAL_KNOWLEDGE.md`, `CA-003_ORGANIZATIONAL_MEMORY_STEWARDSHIP.md`, and `CA-004_CANONICAL_MEMORY_ADAPTATION.md` define the Knowledge Constitution. Approved constitutional amendments belong in the Educational Corpus.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/chief-agent/CHIEF_AGENT_OPERATING_MODEL.md`, and `docs/chief-agent/CHIEF_AGENT_MISSION_SYSTEM.md` define Chief Agent identity, operating loop, approval gates, mission lifecycle, and learning outputs. These belong in the Educational Corpus as operational-governance curriculum.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_ENGINEERING_INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/architecture/KP_ARCHITECTURAL_RECONCILIATION.md`, and `docs/reconciliation/KP-010_CLOSEOUT.md` define knowledge, graph, context, learning, and engineering-intelligence relationships. Approved and reconciled architecture belongs in the Educational Corpus.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/knowledge-governance/EVIDENCE_MODEL.md`, `KNOWLEDGE_INTERMEDIATE_REPRESENTATION.md`, and `CANONICAL_KNOWLEDGE_MODEL.md` define evidence, provisional knowledge, provenance, review, lifecycle, and canonical structure. These belong in the Educational Corpus according to their approved constitutional status and may not override higher authority.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — Repository governance and architecture sources identify approved architecture, blueprints, ADRs, EDRs, specifications, standards, security policy, operational documentation, Runtime documentation, Knowledge Operations documentation, API documentation, user documentation, domain documentation, business documentation, and mission documentation as potential knowledge sources or governing instructions.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — A category may enter the Educational Corpus only when its authority, approval state, ownership, scope, version, and relationship to higher authority are known. Curriculum categories include: Vision 2050; Platform Constitution; approved blueprints and governing architecture; constitutional amendments; final reconciliations; approved ADRs and EDRs; approved specifications; engineering standards; governance; security policy; domain knowledge; business knowledge; user documentation; API documentation; operational and Runtime documentation; Knowledge Operations documentation; and mission documentation.

**UNRESOLVED** — Business knowledge and domain knowledge are repository-supported as potential knowledge categories, but their canonical source registries, approval owners, and minimum curriculum requirements are not uniformly defined.

# Article VII — Documentation Education

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Documentation has distinct constitutional roles and shall not be treated as one undifferentiated source class.

## Documentation as curriculum

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Canon, Constitution, approved amendments, approved governance, governing architecture, approved operating models, approved standards, and approved specifications are curriculum because they instruct the Chief Agent about purpose, authority, boundaries, obligations, and expected behavior.

## Documentation as governance

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/governance/CONSTITUTIONAL_DOCUMENT_GOVERNANCE.md` establishes that document authority depends on class, status, scope, ownership, and supersession. Governing documentation controls education rather than merely contributing evidence.

## Documentation as architectural instruction

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `KORELUMINA_ENGINEERING_INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`, and approved subsystem reconciliations define system relationships, boundaries, and dependency direction. Approved architecture is instructional curriculum.

## Documentation as organizational instruction

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/canon/VISION_2050.md` and Chief Agent operating documents define organizational purpose, human authority, mission operation, and learning expectations. They are organizational instruction.

## Documentation as evidence

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/knowledge-governance/EVIDENCE_MODEL.md`, sections **Document Evidence** and **Initial Evidence Sources**, states that documents may be active guidance, historical evidence, or archived context.

## Documentation as implementation artifact or historical record

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Implementation logs, closeouts, validation reports, roadmaps, historical documents, archived records, and superseded specifications may serve as evidence or history but do not become curriculum unless explicitly approved and scoped by governance.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Documentation enters the lifecycle first according to its authority class. Governing documentation enters the Educational Corpus as curriculum. Non-governing documentation enters as evidence, is compiled and reviewed, and may contribute to Canonical Knowledge or Organizational Memory before educational use.

# Article VIII — Initial Competency

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Initial Competency is the validated pre-activation capability of the Chief Agent to understand KoreLumina’s constitutional purpose, authority hierarchy, architecture, knowledge model, mission system, human approval boundaries, Runtime truth boundary, recovery obligations, and evidence-governed learning lifecycle.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/KORELUMINA_REPOSITORY_KNOWLEDGE_SEEDING_V1.md`, sections **Purpose**, **Objectives**, **Engineer Learning Lifecycle**, and **Long-Term Evolution**, requires initial engineering competency before autonomous learning and defines the expected learning of engineering reasoning, trade-offs, implementation strategies, production constraints, and operational practices.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, sections **Decision Boundaries**, **Human Override**, **Relationship to Runtime**, and **Operating Principles**, defines competencies necessary for safe Chief Agent operation.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Education is constitutionally complete only when:

- the approved Educational Corpus is identified and versioned;
- required curriculum authority and scope are known;
- the Genesis Corpus has been processed through governed knowledge controls;
- Initial Competency has been assessed against approved constitutional domains;
- unresolved conflicts and missing knowledge are visible;
- the Chief Agent can explain governing authority, cite provenance, distinguish knowledge from Runtime truth, preserve human approval, and identify when it lacks authority or knowledge;
- human governance accepts the competency assessment.

**UNRESOLVED** — Numeric competency thresholds, domain-specific scoring, examination methods, and recertification intervals are not constitutionally defined by current governing evidence.

# Article IX — Activation

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Activation is the human-authorized transition from education-only state into operational Chief Agent responsibility.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/canon/VISION_2050.md`, section **Human Leadership**, permanently reserves governance, approval, prioritization, responsibility, and direction to humans.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/chief-agent/CHIEF_AGENT_OPERATING_MODEL.md`, section **Human Approval Gates**, requires human approval for high-impact actions, canonical promotion, architecture changes, destructive actions, deployment, autonomous expansion, and recovery rollback.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Activation requires:

- completed and accepted Initial Competency;
- an approved Educational Corpus version;
- access to governed Canonical Knowledge and Organizational Memory projections;
- operational access constrained by explicit role and mission boundaries;
- visible human override and approval gates;
- Runtime truth integration for operational state;
- observability, auditability, rollback, and recovery paths;
- explicit human authorization by the responsible constitutional authority.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Activation does not grant authority to rewrite constitutional curriculum, promote knowledge to canonical status, fabricate Runtime state, bypass governance, or expand autonomy without approval.

# Article X — Continuous Learning

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`, Laws 6, 8, 9, 11, and 12, requires continuous learning from implementation, failure, repair, deployment, rollback, review, ADRs, incidents, historical work, runtime recovery, and engineering discussions.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/chief-agent/CHIEF_AGENT_OPERATING_MODEL.md`, sections **Learning Workflow** and **Continuous Improvement**, states that every mission produces decisions, lessons, patterns, pitfalls, recovery anchors, and reusable knowledge and that validated engineering work becomes repository-backed institutional memory.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/reconciliation/KP-010_CLOSEOUT.md` defines the Learning Platform as consuming context and producing validated patterns and insights while remaining separate from reasoning, planning, and execution.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Continuous Learning begins after activation and may consume governed evidence from runtime events, engineering work, conversations, missions, deployments, incidents, recoveries, operational outcomes, audits, human corrections, and validated feedback.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Post-activation learning follows the Knowledge Constitution: evidence capture → Knowledge IR → Knowledge Package → validation and required human review → Canonical Knowledge → Organizational Memory adaptation → graph, retrieval, context, learning validation → future use.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Learning output may improve patterns, insights, methods, planning guidance, recovery guidance, and future mission capability. It may not silently alter constitutional curriculum, create canonical authority, override Runtime truth, or bypass human approval.

# Article XI — Educational Governance

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Human constitutional governance approves the curriculum, its authority classes, and all changes to required constitutional education.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — `docs/governance/CONSTITUTIONAL_DOCUMENT_GOVERNANCE.md` assigns authority, approval, ownership, supersession, and conflict-resolution responsibilities according to document class and scope.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Knowledge Platform validates provenance, knowledge state, and corpus traceability. Organizational Memory validates stewarded educational projections. Knowledge Operations exposes review, lifecycle, conflict, and readiness state. None of these systems authorizes activation independently.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The responsible human constitutional authority authorizes activation after competency validation. Educational changes affecting Canon, Constitution, amendments, governance, architecture, or human approval require approval through the authority mechanism governing that document class.

**UNRESOLVED** — The repository does not uniformly name a specific human office or role for curriculum certification and Chief Agent activation across all governing documents.

# Article XII — Engineer Agent ↔ Chief Agent Educational Relationship

**CONFLICT** — `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`, Law 8, defines an Engineer Agent maturity path. `docs/architecture/CHIEF_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` defines a Chief Agent as executive intelligence and platform governor. No prior governing document explicitly resolves whether one contains, replaces, educates, or delegates to the other.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Engineer Agent and Chief Agent share the governed Educational Corpus and Knowledge Constitution but have distinct responsibility scopes.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Engineer Agent’s educational responsibility is engineering competence: understanding architecture, implementation, validation, recovery, standards, patterns, and bounded execution methods.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Chief Agent’s educational responsibility is executive engineering competence: understanding constitutional authority, organizational purpose, mission planning, delegation, governance, knowledge stewardship, Runtime truth, risk, escalation, and human approval boundaries.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Knowledge transfer between them occurs only through governed knowledge, Organizational Memory, mission records, validated learning outputs, and explicit delegation contracts. Conversation context or model state alone is not authoritative transfer.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Chief Agent may delegate bounded engineering tasks to the Engineer Agent or specialist agents, but it does not own their internal learning state. The Engineer Agent may produce evidence and learning outputs, but it does not acquire Chief Agent mission authority or constitutional activation authority.

**UNRESOLVED** — Whether both agents are separate runtime identities, one shared intelligence with separate roles, or an evolutionary succession remains an implementation and future constitutional reconciliation question. This amendment fixes educational and authority boundaries without selecting an implementation topology.

# Article XIII — Constitutional Invariants

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Education precedes activation. Authority: Platform Constitution Law 9 and Repository Knowledge Seeding’s initial-competency requirement.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Learning begins after activation; pre-activation processing is education. Authority: Learning Constitution Reconciliation’s distinction between Genesis, Day-0 processing, Initial Competency, and continuous mission learning.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Governing documentation is curriculum; non-governing documentation is evidence or history unless approved for curriculum. Authority: Constitutional Documentation Governance and Evidence Model.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Canonical Knowledge governs authoritative educational knowledge. Authority: CA-002.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Organizational Memory preserves educational continuity and governed projections but does not determine canonical status. Authority: CA-003 and CA-004.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Operational evidence supplements education and continuous learning but does not override constitutional curriculum. Authority: Platform Constitution Laws 3, 6, 7, 9, and 11.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Runtime truth remains authoritative for operational state. Authority: Chief Agent Architecture, **Relationship to Runtime**.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Human governance approves curriculum, competency acceptance, activation, high-impact actions, and autonomy expansion. Authority: Vision 2050, **Human Leadership**, and Chief Agent Operating Model, **Human Approval Gates**.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Provenance, scope, lifecycle state, conflicts, and supersession remain visible throughout education and learning. Authority: CA-001 through CA-004.

# Article XIV — Compatibility

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Learning Constitution is compatible with the Knowledge Constitution because it consumes the artifact, trust, stewardship, and adaptation contracts of CA-001 through CA-004 without redefining them.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — KPP provides evidence, preservation, compilation, validation, graph, retrieval, context, learning, and memory foundations required by the educational lifecycle. This amendment assigns constitutional lifecycle meaning without redesigning those subsystems.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Organizational Memory remains steward, not educator-authority or canonical authority. It preserves curriculum continuity and educational projections under CA-003 and CA-004.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Chief Agent architecture remains unchanged. This amendment adds pre-activation educational and activation conditions to its existing executive loop.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Runtime remains the operational source of truth and does not become the curriculum authority or learning store.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Knowledge Operations remains the governance and observability workspace for knowledge review, promotion, lifecycle, conflict, and learning status; it does not independently approve activation unless delegated by human constitutional authority.

# Article XV — Remaining Constitutional Gaps

**UNRESOLVED** — Initial Competency requires approved domain standards, assessment methods, scoring, minimum thresholds, failure handling, and recertification policy.

**UNRESOLVED** — Curriculum certification and activation authority require a consistently named human office or role.

**UNRESOLVED** — Domain knowledge and business knowledge require canonical source registries, ownership, privacy, approval, and minimum educational scope.

**UNRESOLVED** — The Engineer Agent and Chief Agent implementation topology remains undefined, although educational and authority boundaries are established.

**UNRESOLVED** — Canonical revocation, supersession, retirement, and propagation authority remains incomplete and affects educational freshness and re-education.

**UNRESOLVED** — The formal constitutional reconciliation that closes the Knowledge and Learning Constitutions and certifies readiness for implementation has not yet been produced.

# Article XVI — Acceptance Criteria

The Learning Constitution is constitutionally accepted only when all of the following are true:

1. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Historical Replay, Genesis Corpus, Repository Knowledge Seeding, Educational Corpus, Initial Competency, Activation, and Continuous Learning are recognized as one ordered constitutional lifecycle.
2. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Education and learning are explicitly distinct, with education preceding activation and learning beginning after activation.
3. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Educational Corpus categories are governed by document authority, approval state, ownership, scope, provenance, and version.
4. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Documentation curriculum is distinguished from documentation evidence, implementation artifacts, and historical records.
5. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Initial Competency and Activation require human approval, visible gaps, Runtime truth boundaries, and governed knowledge access.
6. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Continuous Learning follows the Knowledge Constitution and may not bypass canonical governance or human authority.
7. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Engineer Agent and Chief Agent educational responsibilities and authority boundaries are constitutionally distinct.
8. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Compatibility with KPP, Organizational Memory, Chief Agent architecture, Runtime, and Knowledge Operations is preserved.
9. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — Remaining constitutional gaps are recorded without being silently resolved.
10. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — No implementation or subsystem redesign is authorized by this amendment.
