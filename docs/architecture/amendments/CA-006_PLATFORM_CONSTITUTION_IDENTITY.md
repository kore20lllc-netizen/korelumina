# CA-006 — Platform Constitution Identity

## Amendment identifier

`CA-006`

## Summary

This amendment establishes explicit governance identity for
`docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`.

It resolves the missing owner, scope, and version identity that previously
prevented the Platform Constitution from satisfying governed Knowledge
manufacturing and Educational Corpus requirements.

It does not alter the Platform Constitution's engineering laws, architectural
requirements, authority class, or substantive scope.

## Motivation

The Platform Constitution is already recorded as authoritative platform
constitutional law.

Repository governance also records that its owner is not explicit in the
document body.

The Learning Constitution requires Educational Corpus material to have known
authority, approval state, ownership, scope, version, and relationship to
higher authority.

The Genesis Knowledge manufacturing contract independently requires explicit
authority owner, authority scope, and authority version before approved
documentation may enter automated Knowledge manufacturing.

The absence of explicit identity therefore creates a governance deadlock for a
document that the Learning Constitution expressly requires as curriculum.

## Evidence

- `docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`
  - Status: Authoritative.
  - Governs KoreLumina platform architecture and mandatory engineering law.
- `docs/governance/CONSTITUTIONAL_DOCUMENT_GOVERNANCE.md`
  - Classifies the document as Constitution.
  - Defines its scope as Platform architecture and engineering law.
  - Records its owner as not explicit in the body.
- `docs/architecture/amendments/CA-005_LEARNING_CONSTITUTION.md`
  - Requires the Platform Constitution in the Educational Corpus.
  - Requires curriculum authority, approval state, ownership, scope, and version to be known.
- `docs/constitution/AMENDMENT_PROCESS.md`
  - Requires constitutional evolution to be established through explicit, evidence-driven amendment records.

## Previous wording

The Platform Constitution declared:

```text
## Status

Authoritative.
```

It did not explicitly declare document owner, constitutional scope, or
document version.

## New wording

The Platform Constitution shall explicitly declare:

```text
Status: Authoritative
Owner: Constitutional Office
Scope: Platform
Version: 1.0.0
```

`1.0.0` is the first explicitly governed version assigned to the Platform
Constitution by this amendment. It is not a reconstruction of an undocumented
historical version identifier.

## Governance decision

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Constitutional Office owns the Platform
Constitution as a constitutional governance artifact.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Platform Constitution governs the Platform
scope: KoreLumina platform architecture and mandatory engineering law.

**CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT** — The Platform Constitution is assigned explicit
document version `1.0.0`.

## Approval

Approved as the explicit governance identity record for
`docs/architecture/00_PLATFORM_CONSTITUTION.md`.
