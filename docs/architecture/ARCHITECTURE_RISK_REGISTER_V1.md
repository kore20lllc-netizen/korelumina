# 1. Purpose
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Architecture Risk Register defines how architectural risks are identified,
evaluated, mitigated, and monitored throughout the lifecycle of KoreLumina.

Architecture risk is continuously managed.

Risk management preserves long-term platform integrity.
