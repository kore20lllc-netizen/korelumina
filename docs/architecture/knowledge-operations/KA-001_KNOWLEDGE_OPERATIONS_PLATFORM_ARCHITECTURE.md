# Purpose

Knowledge Operations is the Engineering Intelligence Platform of KoreLumina.

Its responsibility is to transform engineering activity into validated organizational capability.

It is not a document browser.

It is not a graph explorer.

It is not a search engine.

Knowledge Operations is the organizational system responsible for producing Engineering Intelligence.

Every engineering activity ultimately passes through this platform.

---

# Mission

Transform engineering experience into organizational wisdom.

The platform continuously acquires, validates, organizes, explains, and distributes Engineering Intelligence throughout KoreLumina.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

Knowledge Operations owns:

- Genesis reconstruction
- live evidence acquisition
- engineering intelligence
- design intelligence
- organizational intelligence
- institutional memory
- canonical knowledge
- provenance
- governance
- Chief Agent learning
