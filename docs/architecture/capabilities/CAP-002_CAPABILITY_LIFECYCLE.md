Status: Draft

Authority:

---

# Purpose

This document defines the lifecycle of every capability in KoreLumina.

Capabilities are living organizational assets.

Like engineering organizations, they evolve through continuous learning.

Capabilities are never considered finished.

They continuously mature.

---

# Core Principle

Capabilities evolve.

Implementations change.

Experiences improve.

Intelligence compounds.

Governance remains.

---

---

# Stage 1 — Need

A capability begins when the organization recognizes a recurring need.

Sources include:

• executive strategy

• engineering pain

• user feedback

• runtime observations

• organizational learning

• Vision evolution

Need always precedes implementation.

---

# Stage 2 — Discovery

Discovery answers:

What problem exists?

Who experiences it?

What evidence supports it?

What intelligence already exists?

Has KoreLumina solved this before?

Discovery prevents duplicate capabilities.

---

# Stage 3 — Definition

Definition establishes:

Purpose

Scope

Consumers

Dependencies

Expected outcomes

Governance

Success metrics

Capability ownership

Definition becomes the constitutional contract.

---

# Stage 4 — Architecture

Architecture defines:

organizational role

capability boundaries

relationships

consumed intelligence

produced intelligence

experience contracts

service boundaries

Architecture stabilizes capability identity.

---

# Stage 5 — Experience Design

The experience is designed before implementation.

The experience defines:

user workflows

decision points

visibility

explainability

operational clarity

The UI is the current implementation of the experience.

---

# Stage 6 — Implementation

Implementation realizes the capability.

Implementation may change.

Capability identity does not.

Multiple implementations may exist.

---

# Stage 7 — Validation

Validation confirms:

engineering correctness

experience quality

runtime stability

governance compliance

organizational value

knowledge generation

Validation determines readiness.

---

# Stage 8 — Operation

The capability enters production.

Operational metrics include:

adoption

quality

performance

reliability

engineering value

organizational value

learning contribution

Operation creates evidence.

---

# Stage 9 — Observation

Observation continuously captures:

user behavior

runtime behavior

engineering outcomes

mission outcomes

organizational outcomes

Everything becomes evidence.

---

# Stage 10 — Learning

Observation produces:

Experience Intelligence

Engineering Intelligence

Design Intelligence

Organizational Intelligence

Strategic Intelligence

Learning changes future capability evolution.

---

# Stage 11 — Evolution

Capabilities continuously improve.

Evolution may include:

new workflows

improved experiences

expanded intelligence

automation

optimization

organizational refinement

Evolution is expected.

---

# Stage 12 — Maturity

A mature capability demonstrates:

predictable quality

high adoption

organizational trust

stable governance

high engineering value

continuous learning

Maturity is never permanent.

---

# Stage 13 — Legacy

Legacy capabilities continue creating value while preparing successors.

Knowledge is preserved.

Consumers migrate gradually.

Legacy protects organizational continuity.

---

# Stage 14 — Retirement

Retirement removes implementation.

It never removes organizational knowledge.

Retirement preserves:

history

evidence

lessons

metrics

relationships

engineering patterns

design evolution

organizational learning

Retirement completes the lifecycle.

---

---

# Success Criteria

The lifecycle succeeds when:

• capabilities evolve without losing identity

• implementations remain replaceable

• experiences continuously improve

• knowledge compounds

• engineering quality increases

• organizational capability grows

• retirement never destroys organizational learning

---

# Long-Term Outcome

Every capability becomes a continuously improving organizational asset.

KoreLumina evolves through capability evolution rather than feature accumulation.

Capabilities become the permanent language of the engineering organization.
