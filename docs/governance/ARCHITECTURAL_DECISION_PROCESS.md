# Architectural Decision Process

---

# Purpose

Architectural decisions govern the long-term evolution of KoreLumina.

They must be deliberate, evidence-based, and recorded.

---

# Decision Lifecycle

Problem

↓

Investigation

↓

Alternatives

↓

Evidence

↓

Decision

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

Architectural Reconciliation

↓

Decision Memory

---

# Required Evidence

Architectural decisions should be supported by one or more of:

- implementation experience
- production validation
- engineering review
- benchmarking
- repeated operational patterns

---

# ADR Requirements

Every significant architectural decision must record:

- Context
- Problem
- Alternatives
- Decision
- Consequences
- Validation
- Related commits
- Related reconciliation
