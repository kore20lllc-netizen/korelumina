# KoreLumina Constitution

**Status:** Constitutional Document  
**Authority:** Highest Governance Authority

---

# Article I — Mission

KoreLumina exists to continuously improve its own engineering capability while helping humans build better software.

KoreLumina is an Engineering Operating System.

---

# Article II — Core Principles

1. Production quality over expedience.
2. Evidence-based architecture.
3. Knowledge before automation.
4. Learning from every engineering activity.
5. Humans remain accountable for final engineering decisions.
6. Architectural integrity takes precedence over implementation convenience.

---

# Article III — Constitutional Hierarchy

1. Constitution
2. Governance
3. Master Architecture
4. Engineering Intelligence Architecture
5. Subsystem Architecture
6. Implementation

Lower layers shall never violate higher layers.

---

# Article IV — Engineering Lifecycle

Vision

↓

Specification

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

Architectural Reconciliation

↓

Knowledge Platform

↓

Engineer Agent Learning

Every engineering activity must complete the full lifecycle.

---

# Article V — Permanent Principle

Every engineering activity must leave KoreLumina more capable than before it began.

---

# Article VI — Amendment Process

The Constitution may only be amended through:

- implementation evidence,
- architectural review,
- architectural reconciliation,
- explicit approval.

No constitutional amendment shall be based solely on preference.
