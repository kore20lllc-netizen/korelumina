# KoreLumina Engineering Principles

---

## Principle 1 — Production First

Every implementation is production-grade by default.

---

## Principle 2 — Correctness Before Performance

Correctness always precedes optimization.

---

## Principle 3 — Architecture Before Convenience

Protect architecture before implementation.

---

## Principle 4 — Capability-Driven Development

Infrastructure exists to enable production capability.

Never build speculative frameworks.

---

## Principle 5 — Evidence-Based Architecture

Architecture evolves through implementation evidence.

Not personal preference.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Learning

Every engineering activity produces reusable knowledge.

---

## Principle 7 — Stable Interfaces

Public contracts evolve conservatively.

---

## Principle 8 — Orchestration Over Coupling

Subsystems communicate through orchestration boundaries.

---

## Principle 9 — Knowledge Is a Product

Engineering knowledge is treated as a first-class asset.

---

## Principle 10 — Reconciliation Is Mandatory

Engineering work is incomplete until architectural reconciliation has been completed.

---

# Engineering Success

Engineering success is measured by:

- software quality
- architectural integrity
- accumulated engineering intelligence
- improvement of the Engineer Agent
