# Roadmap Governance

---

# Purpose

The roadmap defines engineering priorities.

It is governed by evidence rather than preference.

---

# Roadmap Hierarchy

Vision

↓

Constitution

↓

Master Architecture

↓

Engineering Intelligence Architecture

↓

Subsystem Roadmaps

↓

Implementation Tickets

---

# Roadmap Evolution

Roadmaps evolve through:

- implementation
- validation
- architectural reconciliation

Every approved improvement shall be reconciled back into the roadmap.

---

# Drift Policy

Drift is not automatically failure.

Every deviation must be classified as:

- Planned
- Approved Improvement
- Premature Implementation
- Regression

Only Approved Improvements become part of future architecture.

---

# Epic Closure

An epic closes only after:

- implementation,
- validation,
- reconciliation,
- roadmap update,
- Knowledge Platform ingestion.
