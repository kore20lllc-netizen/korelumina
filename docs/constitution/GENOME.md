# KoreLumina Genome

## Purpose

The KoreLumina Genome represents the accumulated engineering capability of the organization.

Unlike source code, which represents the current implementation, the Genome represents everything the organization has learned while building, operating, governing, recovering, and evolving the platform.

The Genome is the long-term strategic asset of KoreLumina.

---

# Philosophy

Every engineering organization possesses a form of institutional DNA.

Most organizations allow it to remain fragmented across:

- source code
- documentation
- engineers
- incident reports
- tribal knowledge
- architectural decisions

KoreLumina intentionally unifies these assets into a single continuously evolving organizational Genome.

---

# Definition

The Genome is the complete body of validated engineering capability accumulated by KoreLumina.

It represents what the organization knows how to do.

It includes:

- architectural capability
- engineering capability
- operational capability
- governance capability
- knowledge capability
- organizational capability

The Genome continuously evolves.

The Constitution preserves its identity.

# Genome Evolution

The Genome evolves through validated engineering experience.

Examples include:

- successful implementations
- architectural improvements
- recovered failures
- operational insights
- engineering discoveries
- governance improvements

Unvalidated assumptions do not become Genome knowledge.

# Relationship to the Knowledge Platform

The Knowledge Platform manages engineering knowledge.

The Genome represents the accumulated organizational capability produced by that knowledge.

The Knowledge Platform is infrastructure.

The Genome is the long-term outcome.

---

# Relationship to Engineering Intelligence

Engineering Intelligence consumes the Genome.

Engineering Intelligence also strengthens the Genome through:

- pattern discovery
- architectural learning
- decision analysis
- operational feedback
- mission retrospectives

The relationship is continuous.

---

# Relationship to the Chief Agent

The Chief Agent should understand the current state of the Genome.

Executive recommendations should consider:

- capability maturity
- knowledge gaps
- organizational strengths
- operational weaknesses
- strategic opportunities

The Genome becomes the organization's memory and capability model.

---

# Stewardship

Every contributor is responsible for strengthening the Genome.

Every mission should identify:

- what new knowledge was created
- what capability improved
- what organizational lesson was learned
- what engineering pattern emerged
- what recovery knowledge was preserved

Genome stewardship is continuous.

---

# Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective of KoreLumina is to create an engineering organization whose capability compounds indefinitely.

Future engineers should inherit a stronger Genome than the one inherited by previous generations.

The Genome should become increasingly capable regardless of changes in technology, programming languages, frameworks, or AI models.

---

# Genome Principle

The KoreLumina Genome is the cumulative engineering capability of the organization.

It is strengthened through every validated mission.

It preserves what the organization has learned.

It guides what the organization should build next.

Protecting and continuously improving the Genome is a constitutional responsibility shared by every contributor.
