# KoreLumina Values

## Purpose

This document defines the enduring values that guide every engineering decision, organizational practice, and strategic investment within KoreLumina.

Values describe how we choose to operate when multiple valid engineering paths exist.

They guide judgment rather than implementation.

---

# Enduring Values

The following values are permanent organizational commitments.

## Engineering Excellence

We pursue engineering excellence over engineering expedience.

Quality, maintainability, recoverability, and long-term capability take precedence over short-term convenience.

---

## Knowledge Preservation

Knowledge is one of KoreLumina's most valuable strategic assets.

Every validated lesson should become institutional knowledge.

Knowledge should compound across missions rather than disappear between them.

---

## Architectural Integrity

Architecture exists to provide long-term stability.

Architectural decisions should be deliberate, explainable, and protected from unnecessary drift.

---

## Responsible Innovation

Innovation is encouraged when it strengthens the organization.

Innovation should never compromise governance, explainability, recoverability, or long-term maintainability.

---

## Continuous Learning

Every mission should improve the organization.

Learning should become visible through:

- improved engineering methods
- better documentation
- reusable knowledge
- stronger governance
- improved engineering capability

---

## Evidence-Based Engineering

Engineering decisions should be supported by evidence whenever practical.

Evidence includes:

- operational data
- validation
- engineering experience
- documented decisions
- measurable outcomes

Assumptions should become evidence before becoming institutional knowledge.

---

## Human Responsibility

Artificial intelligence enhances engineering capability.

Human leadership remains responsible for:

- governance
- ethics
- architecture
- strategic direction
- organizational stewardship

Responsibility is never delegated.

---

## Transparency

Engineering decisions should be understandable.

Architecture should be explainable.

Governance should be visible.

Knowledge should be discoverable.

Transparency strengthens trust.

---

## Recoverability

Recoverability is a design principle.

Every important capability should be observable, diagnosable, and recoverable.

Failures should strengthen future engineering capability.

---

## Organizational Stewardship

Every contributor is a steward of KoreLumina.

Stewardship includes:

- preserving institutional knowledge
- improving governance
- protecting architectural integrity
- strengthening future engineering capability

Every contribution should leave the organization stronger than it was before.

---

# Decision Filter

When multiple engineering choices are available, prefer the option that best strengthens:

- engineering capability
- organizational capability
- institutional memory
- governance maturity
- architectural integrity
- long-term sustainability

---

# Values Statement

KoreLumina values engineering excellence, preserved knowledge, disciplined governance, responsible innovation, continuous learning, evidence-based decision making, transparency, recoverability, and long-term organizational stewardship.

These values guide every engineering decision across the organization.
