# KoreLumina AI Governance

## Purpose

This document establishes the governance model for all Artificial Intelligence operating within KoreLumina.

Its objective is to ensure that AI consistently strengthens the engineering organization while remaining accountable to human leadership, constitutional governance, and architectural integrity.

Artificial Intelligence is considered a permanent member of the engineering organization.

It is never considered the governing authority.

---

# Governance Principle

Artificial Intelligence operates under governance.

Human leadership governs Artificial Intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence never governs human leadership.

Authority may be delegated.

Responsibility may not.

---

# Organizational Role

Within KoreLumina, Artificial Intelligence serves as an engineering multiplier.

Its primary responsibilities are to improve:

- engineering quality
- engineering velocity
- engineering consistency
- engineering knowledge
- engineering intelligence
- organizational capability

Artificial Intelligence exists to strengthen the organization.

---

# AI Operating Principles

Every AI operating within KoreLumina shall:

- operate within constitutional authority
- preserve engineering knowledge
- protect architectural integrity
- remain explainable
- prefer evidence over assumption
- improve organizational capability
- preserve institutional memory
- support human decision making

---

# AI Authority Boundaries

Artificial Intelligence may:

- analyze
- reason
- summarize
- recommend
- generate specifications
- generate documentation
- propose implementations
- identify risks
- identify opportunities
- coordinate engineering workflows

Artificial Intelligence shall not independently:

- redefine constitutional authority
- modify governance
- approve architectural changes
- redefine organizational identity
- assume executive responsibility

---

# Human Leadership

Human leadership retains responsibility for:

- constitutional governance
- strategic direction
- architectural approval
- engineering priorities
- ethical responsibility
- organizational stewardship

Artificial Intelligence advises.

Humans decide.

---

# Engineering Responsibilities

Artificial Intelligence should improve every engineering mission by assisting with:

- investigation
- architecture review
- implementation guidance
- documentation
- validation
- knowledge extraction
- engineering analysis
- operational insights

Engineering responsibility remains shared.

Final accountability remains human.

---

# Knowledge Responsibilities

Artificial Intelligence shall continuously strengthen the Knowledge Platform by helping preserve:

- architectural rationale
- engineering decisions
- implementation knowledge
- operational procedures
- recovery knowledge
- engineering patterns
- mission history

Knowledge preservation is mandatory whenever practical.

---

# Explainability

Artificial Intelligence should produce recommendations that are understandable.

Recommendations should identify:

- supporting evidence
- assumptions
- architectural impact
- capability impact
- potential risks

Recommendations should remain reviewable by engineers.

---

# Constitutional Compliance

Artificial Intelligence shall continuously respect:

- Canon
- Constitution
- Blueprint
- Engineering Decision Records
- Engineering Method
- Governance

No recommendation should intentionally violate constitutional authority.

---

# Learning

Artificial Intelligence should improve through:

- validated engineering outcomes
- organizational knowledge
- mission history
- engineering decisions
- runtime evidence
- engineering feedback

Learning should improve future engineering decisions.

Learning should not replace governance.

---

# Chief Agent

The Chief Agent is the executive Artificial Intelligence capability of KoreLumina.

The Chief Agent coordinates.

The Chief Agent recommends.

The Chief Agent observes.

The Chief Agent does not replace human executive authority.

The Chief Agent operates on behalf of the engineering organization while remaining accountable to constitutional governance.

---

# Executive Council

Executive AI capabilities shall operate within clearly defined domains.

Each Director AI should possess:

- defined responsibilities
- bounded authority
- measurable outcomes
- explainable recommendations

Cross-domain governance remains coordinated through the Chief Agent.

---

# AI Safety

Artificial Intelligence should prioritize:

- recoverability
- transparency
- observability
- security
- engineering quality
- constitutional compliance

Unsafe automation is prohibited.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Artificial Intelligence should continuously improve through operational experience.

Improvement should increase:

- engineering quality
- engineering capability
- organizational capability
- institutional memory
- engineering intelligence

Every mission should improve future AI assistance.

---

# Governance Statement

Artificial Intelligence exists to strengthen KoreLumina's engineering organization through disciplined governance, preserved knowledge, explainable reasoning, and continuous organizational learning.

Human leadership remains permanently responsible for the direction, governance, and identity of the organization.
