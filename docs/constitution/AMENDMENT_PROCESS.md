# KoreLumina Constitutional Amendment Process

## Purpose

The Constitution defines the permanent engineering principles governing KoreLumina.

Because the Constitution sits above governance, architecture, and implementation, amendments must be rare, deliberate, and evidence-driven.

Constitutional stability is essential to preserving the long-term identity of the platform.

---

# Constitutional Hierarchy

Constitution

↓

Governance

↓

Architecture

↓

Implementation

Lower layers may evolve frequently.

The Constitution should evolve only when fundamental engineering principles change.

---

# When an Amendment is Required

An amendment is required when a proposal changes:

- Mission
- Engineering philosophy
- Core engineering principles
- Constitutional hierarchy
- Platform purpose
- Definition of engineering success

Architectural refinements do not require constitutional amendments.

# Evidence Requirements

Constitutional amendments require strong evidence.

Acceptable evidence includes:

- repeated implementation experience
- architectural reconciliation
- production validation
- long-term engineering observations
- governance review

Preference alone is insufficient.

---

# Approval Requirements

Every amendment must satisfy:

- Constitutional consistency
- Engineering benefit
- Long-term maintainability
- Platform vision alignment
- Production evidence

---

# Amendment Record

Every amendment shall record:

- Amendment identifier
- Summary
- Motivation
- Evidence
- Previous wording
- New wording
- Related RFC
- Related ADR
- Related Reconciliation
- Approval date

The Constitution itself should remain concise.

Historical evolution is preserved through amendment records.

---

# Relationship to the Knowledge Platform

Every constitutional amendment becomes:

- Governance Knowledge
- Decision Memory
- Engineering Memory

The Engineer Agent shall learn not only the current Constitution, but also its evolution over time.
