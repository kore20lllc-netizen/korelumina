# The KoreLumina Constitution

## Preamble

The KoreLumina Constitution is the supreme governing document of the KoreLumina Engineering Organization.

Its purpose is to preserve the identity, governance, engineering principles, organizational structure, and long-term integrity of KoreLumina while allowing continuous evolution through disciplined engineering.

The Constitution exists to ensure KoreLumina continuously improves without losing its purpose.

Every contributor—human or artificial intelligence—operates under its authority.

---

# Article I — Purpose

KoreLumina exists to advance Engineering Intelligence.

Engineering Intelligence is achieved through the disciplined integration of:

- Human Expertise
- Institutional Knowledge
- Engineering Governance
- Validated Experience
- Artificial Intelligence

The primary product of KoreLumina is continuously improving engineering capability.

Software is one expression of that capability.

---

# Article II — Authority

Authority within KoreLumina flows through the following hierarchy.

```
Human Leadership
        ↓
Canon
        ↓
Constitution
        ↓
Blueprint
        ↓
Engineering Decision Records
        ↓
Architecture
        ↓
Specifications
        ↓
Implementation
```

No lower authority may contradict a higher authority.

---

# Article III — Organization

KoreLumina operates as an engineering organization.

Its organizational model consists of:

- Human Leadership
- Chief Agent
- Executive Council
- Engineering Division
- Knowledge Division
- Operations Division
- Runtime Services
- Platform Capabilities

The organization exists to continuously improve engineering capability.

---

# Article IV — Engineering Principles

Engineering within KoreLumina shall be governed by the following principles.

- Engineering Intelligence First
- Governance Before Automation
- Knowledge Before Repetition
- Evidence Before Assumption
- Recovery Before Convenience
- Documentation as Infrastructure
- Human Responsibility Remains Final
- Continuous Organizational Improvement
- Architectural Integrity
- Explainable Engineering

These principles govern every engineering decision.

---

# Article V — Knowledge

Knowledge is a strategic organizational asset.

Engineering knowledge shall be:

- preserved
- validated
- organized
- discoverable
- explainable
- reusable

Institutional knowledge shall receive the same engineering discipline as production software.

No validated engineering knowledge should be intentionally discarded.

---

# Article VI — Governance

Governance exists to preserve constitutional integrity while enabling disciplined evolution.

Governance shall:

- prevent architectural drift
- preserve organizational identity
- maintain traceability
- improve engineering quality
- protect strategic assets

Major architectural changes require documented review.

---

# Article VII — Engineering Method

Every engineering mission follows the KoreLumina Engineering Method.

```
Vision

↓

Mission

↓

Architecture

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

Documentation

↓

Knowledge Preservation

↓

Engineering Intelligence

↓

Genome Evolution
```

No engineering mission is complete until knowledge has been preserved.

---

# Article VIII — Strategic Assets

The following are strategic organizational assets.

- Constitution
- Blueprint
- Engineering Method
- Knowledge Platform
- Chief Agent
- Engineering Intelligence
- Organizational Memory
- KoreLumina Genome

These assets receive long-term stewardship.

---

# Article IX — Engineering Intelligence

Engineering Intelligence exists to improve the engineering organization.

It operates through:

- validated knowledge
- engineering governance
- organizational memory
- runtime evidence
- engineering decisions
- mission history

Engineering Intelligence advises.

Human leadership governs.

---

# Article X — KoreLumina Genome

The KoreLumina Genome represents the accumulated engineering capability of the organization.

It includes:

- architectural knowledge
- engineering patterns
- engineering decisions
- operational experience
- institutional memory
- governance
- recovery knowledge
- mission history

Every completed mission should strengthen the Genome.

---

# Article XI — Continuous Evolution

KoreLumina is intentionally evolutionary.

Evolution shall occur through:

- validated engineering experience
- preserved organizational knowledge
- disciplined governance
- measurable capability improvement

Architectural evolution shall remain consistent with the Constitution.

---

# Article XII — Human Responsibility

Artificial Intelligence augments engineering capability.

Human leadership retains responsibility for:

- governance
- architecture
- organizational direction
- ethical responsibility
- strategic decisions

Authority may be delegated.

Responsibility may not.

---

# Article XIII — Constitutional Amendment

The Constitution may evolve only when the long-term interests of KoreLumina require it.

Constitutional amendments require:

- documented rationale
- architectural review
- governance review
- approval by repository leadership
- preservation of historical intent

Constitutional amendments should be rare.

---

# Constitutional Oath

Every contributor accepts the following commitment.

I will preserve knowledge before convenience.

I will favor evidence over assumption.

I will strengthen the engineering organization.

I will document decisions that matter.

I will build recoverable systems.

I will contribute to institutional memory.

I will leave KoreLumina more capable than I found it.

---

# Constitutional Principle

The Constitution exists to preserve KoreLumina's identity while enabling continuous engineering evolution.

Every engineering decision should strengthen the organization without compromising its constitutional foundations.
