# KoreLumina Constitution

## Purpose

The Constitution defines how KoreLumina is governed.

Where the Canon defines who KoreLumina is, the Constitution defines how KoreLumina operates.

It establishes the permanent governance framework that guides every engineering decision, architectural evolution, organizational responsibility, and implementation.

The Constitution exists to ensure KoreLumina evolves without losing its identity.

---

# Constitutional Hierarchy

```
Canon
    ↓
Constitution
    ↓
Blueprint
    ↓
Engineering Decision Records
    ↓
Architecture
    ↓
Specifications
    ↓
Implementation
```

Every lower level inherits authority from the level above it.

---

# Constitutional Scope

The Constitution governs:

- organizational structure
- engineering governance
- engineering method
- architectural authority
- knowledge governance
- decision making
- capability ownership
- continuous evolution

It does not define implementation details.

# Constitutional Principles

The Constitution exists to preserve:

- architectural integrity
- organizational continuity
- engineering excellence
- institutional knowledge
- disciplined governance
- responsible evolution

These principles remain stable across technologies, implementations, and products.

---

# Governance

Constitutional changes require:

- documented rationale
- architectural review
- approval by repository leadership
- preservation of historical intent

Constitutional documents evolve deliberately.

---

# Relationship to the Blueprint

The Blueprint translates constitutional principles into architectural structure.

The Constitution answers:

"How should KoreLumina operate?"

The Blueprint answers:

"How is KoreLumina built?"

---

# Relationship to Engineering Decisions

Engineering Decision Records implement constitutional intent.

EDRs never redefine constitutional authority.

---

# Constitutional Stewardship

The Constitution is maintained on behalf of future contributors.

Every engineer and every AI operating within KoreLumina inherits its authority before contributing to the platform.

---

# Constitutional Principle

KoreLumina shall evolve continuously while preserving its constitutional identity.

The Constitution exists to make that possible.
