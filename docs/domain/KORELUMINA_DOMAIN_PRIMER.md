## Domains

KoreLumina works in two connected domains.

### 1. AI-assisted application engineering

People use AI to create, import, run, analyze, repair, and deploy software.

Most real software already exists. Customers bring inherited repositories, legacy systems, monorepos, mixed-language stacks, and technical debt, not only new projects.

KoreLumina's Builder, Runtime, and Preview serve this domain: a workspace where software is imported, understood, changed, previewed, and shipped with AI assistance, with human engineers available when AI is not enough.

### 2. Engineering organization

How engineering work is planned, governed, remembered, and improved over time.

KoreLumina's Knowledge Platform, Chief Agent, and Master OS serve this domain: engineering knowledge is preserved, decisions are governed, and capability compounds across missions.

KoreLumina treats these as one problem: better software comes from a better engineering organization.

## How KoreLumina Is Different

**An operating system, not a code generator.** KoreLumina connects the customer's repository, AI, infrastructure, and deployment into one governed workspace. It works on the software customers already have.

**KoreLumina never owns the customer's software.** Code, data, keys, infrastructure, and deployment path stay with the customer. Projects stay portable, and customers can leave at any time.

**Open AI choice.** Customers can use AI included in their plan, or bring their own AI provider keys and pay the provider directly.

**Customers are not forced into platform AI billing.** Many platforms in this space require customers to buy all AI usage through the platform. With KoreLumina, a customer can use the workspace with their own AI.

**The workspace is the product.** Value comes from the environment, governance, knowledge, and engineering expertise KoreLumina provides, not from reselling AI usage.

**AI plus experts.** When AI reaches its limit, KoreLumina engineers take over from inside the same workspace.

**The customer decides spending.** Costly operations are shown as an execution plan with estimates and rollback before anything runs.

## Domain Terms

- **Workspace**: the environment a customer uses to import, build, run, analyze, and deploy software on KoreLumina.
- **Repository import**: bringing an existing project into KoreLumina by ZIP, GitHub URL, or connected repository.
- **Repository intelligence**: indexing a repository's structure, dependencies, and conventions so AI works with real context.
- **Repository analysis**: inspecting a customer's repository to identify issues and explain them.
- **Repository repair**: fixing the identified issues when the customer asks.
- **Runtime preview**: running a project in an isolated environment and previewing changes before they are applied.
- **Managed infrastructure**: KoreLumina hosts and runs the customer's project.
- **Bring your own infrastructure (BYO)**: the customer connects their own GitHub, Supabase, cloud, and deployment target.
- **Bring your own AI keys (BYO AI)**: the customer connects their own AI provider account and pays that provider directly.
- **AI credits / AI usage**: AI model usage included with a plan, with usage-based billing beyond the included amount.
- **Expert escalation**: handing a task from AI to KoreLumina engineers inside the workspace without losing context.
- **Engineering service**: work delivered by KoreLumina engineers, such as audits, repair, modernization, migration, or dedicated development.
- **Execution plan**: the estimate of credits, cost, time, models, infrastructure impact, artifacts, and rollback shown to a customer before a costly operation.
- **Lock-in**: any arrangement that makes it hard for a customer to take their software elsewhere. KoreLumina avoids it by design.

## Standards and Constraints

No industry-specific regulatory constraints are currently declared.

Enterprise customers expect access control, SSO, audit trails, and compliance support. No formal certification is declared in this primer; the Chief Agent must not claim one.

If KoreLumina enters a regulated domain (for example, healthcare or finance), this primer must be updated before the Chief Agent plans missions in that domain.
