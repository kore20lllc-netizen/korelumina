# KoreLumina Blueprint

## Purpose

The KoreLumina Blueprint is the canonical architectural map of the platform.

It defines the organizational structure, platform domains, strategic capabilities, execution programs, and architectural boundaries that govern every implementation.

The Blueprint answers one question:

> If KoreLumina were rebuilt from nothing, what would be built, in what order, and why?

Every Engineering Decision Record (EDR), architectural specification, implementation, and production capability must remain traceable to this document.

---

# Architectural Authority

The Blueprint is governed by the KoreLumina Constitution.

Authority flows through the organization as follows.

```
Constitution
        ↓
Blueprint
        ↓
Engineering Decision Records
        ↓
Architecture
        ↓
Specifications
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Knowledge Preservation
```

No implementation may contradict the Blueprint.

# Architectural Principles

The Blueprint is governed by the following principles.

1. Organization before implementation.
2. Governance before automation.
3. Knowledge before repetition.
4. Evidence before assumption.
5. Recovery before convenience.
6. Human responsibility remains final.
7. Every mission strengthens the organization.
8. Every capability is observable.
9. Every implementation is traceable.
10. Every architectural change requires governance.

---

# Traceability

Every implementation must identify:

- Blueprint section
- Capability
- Engineering Decision Record
- Specification
- Validation
- Knowledge produced

Traceability is mandatory.

---

# Architecture Freeze

Architecture Baseline 1.0 is frozen.

The following require an approved Engineering Decision Record:

- new platform domains
- constitutional changes
- organizational restructuring
- operating model changes
- new strategic assets

Capability maturation does not require architectural redesign.

---

# Success Criteria

The Blueprint is successful when:

- every subsystem belongs to a defined domain
- every capability has an owner
- every Engineering Decision Record references the Blueprint
- every implementation is traceable
- organizational capability continuously improves
- architectural drift is prevented

---

# Blueprint Principle

The Blueprint exists to preserve architectural integrity while enabling continuous engineering evolution.

Every implementation should strengthen KoreLumina's engineering capability without compromising its constitutional identity.
