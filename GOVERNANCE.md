# KoreLumina Governance

## Purpose

Governance ensures KoreLumina evolves deliberately while preserving its constitutional identity, architectural integrity, engineering quality, and institutional knowledge.

The objective of governance is not to slow engineering.

It is to ensure every engineering decision strengthens the organization.

---

# Governance Objectives

Governance exists to:

- preserve architectural integrity
- prevent architectural drift
- maintain traceability
- ensure engineering quality
- preserve institutional knowledge
- guide long-term evolution
- protect strategic assets

---

# Order of Authority

Every engineering decision follows this order of authority.

```
Constitution
        ↓
Blueprint
        ↓
Engineering Decision Records
        ↓
Architecture
        ↓
Specifications
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Knowledge Preservation
```

Lower levels may extend higher levels.

They may never contradict them.

---

# Architecture Baseline

Architecture Baseline 1.0 is frozen.

New foundational concepts require:

- Engineering Decision Record
- Architecture Review
- Constitutional approval

Capability maturation does not require architectural redesign.

---

# Engineering Decision Records

Every architectural decision shall include:

- Problem Statement
- Context
- Alternatives Considered
- Decision
- Rationale
- Consequences
- Related Capabilities
- Blueprint References
- Implementation Impact

EDRs become permanent institutional knowledge.

---

# Architecture Review Board

Every architectural proposal shall be reviewed against:

- Constitutional alignment
- Blueprint consistency
- Capability impact
- Organizational impact
- Knowledge impact
- Recovery impact
- Operational readiness
- Security considerations

For KoreLumina today, the repository owner serves as the Architecture Review Board.

Future implementations may delegate preparation to the Chief Agent, but final approval remains human.

---

# Capability Governance

Every capability must define:

- Capability ID
- Owner
- Division
- Current maturity
- Target maturity
- Dependencies
- Success metrics
- Blueprint reference
- Related Engineering Decisions

Capabilities are organizational assets.

---

# Documentation Governance

Documentation is production infrastructure.

Every major document shall include metadata describing:

- title
- status
- owner
- authority
- version
- review cycle
- related documents

Documentation changes follow the same governance process as source code.

---

# Repository Governance

Every completed mission shall leave behind:

- validated implementation
- updated documentation
- preserved engineering knowledge
- recovery guidance
- architectural traceability

No mission is complete until organizational capability has improved.

---

# Definition of Done

A mission is complete only when:

- implementation is complete
- validation has passed
- documentation is updated
- recovery guidance exists
- knowledge has been preserved
- Blueprint traceability exists
- capability maturity has increased where applicable

---

# Change Management

Every significant change shall answer:

1. What problem is being solved?
2. Which capability improves?
3. Which Blueprint section applies?
4. Which EDR authorizes the change?
5. What knowledge is preserved?
6. How is success validated?

---

# Knowledge Preservation

Every completed mission contributes to:

- Institutional Memory
- Knowledge Platform
- Engineering Intelligence
- KoreLumina Genome

Knowledge preservation is mandatory.

---

# Continuous Improvement

KoreLumina measures progress by improvements in:

- engineering capability
- organizational capability
- knowledge quality
- governance maturity
- operational excellence

Feature count is not the primary measure of success.

---

# Governance Principle

Governance exists to ensure KoreLumina continuously improves without losing its architectural identity.

Every engineering decision should leave the organization stronger than it was before.
